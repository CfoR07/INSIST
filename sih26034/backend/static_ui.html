INSIST

Automated Pre-Packed Commodity Compliance & Verification Assistant

Developed for Smart India Hackathon (SIH26034), INSIST bridges the gap between field enforcement officers and complex statutory laws under the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011.

The Problem We Are Solving
  • Manual Inspection Bottleneck: Field officers manually review dozens of tiny statutory declarations per package, creating massive delays.
  • Missed Statutory Violations: Missing Unit Sale Price (USP), ambiguous MRP without tax declarations, or incorrect font sizes are frequently overlooked.
  • Lack of Verifiable Provenance: Traditional paper audits lack tamper-evident timestamping and photographic coordinate evidence.
The INSIST Solution & Bridge
  • AI Reads, Code Decides: Multi-pass native OCR extracts raw text without LLM hallucinations. Pure deterministic code enforces Rule 6 & Rule 9 compliance.
  • OpenCV Quality Gate: Laplacian variance checks guarantee packaging photos are sharp and glare-free before legal evaluation.
  • Instant Tamper-Evident Certificates: Digitally signed inspection reports with SHA-256 integrity hashes and one-click PDF export.

Core System Architecture

Layer 01 Live Multi-Angle Capture Webcam, mobile lens, or high-res photo tray with OpenCV quality filtering.
Layer 02 Multi-Pass OCR Engine Multi-angle sweeps, CLAHE normalization, and contour-based Veg/Non-Veg logo detection.
Layer 03 Deterministic Rule Matrix Auditable, non-hallucinatory evaluation against versioned LMPC 2011 statutory rules.
